# Updates to Our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

We are updating our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to support joining up to three families or groups with one account.

* You can set a separate profile for each family. Information about your other families and your membership in them is not shared between families.
* You can receive notifications from your families on the devices registered to your account and choose which family each widget displays.
* To send reminders at the times you choose, we process your device's time zone name. We do not collect location coordinates for this purpose and keep only the latest time zone value.
* We clarify what happens when you leave a family or delete your account, what is stored on your device, and how notification processing records are retained.

Existing users do not need to give consent again for this update. If you do not agree to the changes, you may end your service agreement by deleting your account in Account settings or contact us. New users should review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy presented during sign-up.

Before the effective date, you can view the currently effective documents separately from the upcoming versions. Feature availability may vary with the app update and release schedule.
